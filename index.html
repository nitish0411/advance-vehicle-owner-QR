How Scanning Works

When someone scans the QR code sticker placed on your car windshield or window:

  • Zero Privacy Leakage: Your personal phone number, full name, and address are completely hidden.
  • 1-Tap Fast Actions: Scanners can report wrong parking, open windows, lights on, or flat tyres in seconds.
  • Masked Voice Relay: Scanners can talk to you directly through encrypted in-browser calling.
  • Emergency Family SOS: In serious emergencies, bystander scans notify you and your emergency family contacts simultaneously.
